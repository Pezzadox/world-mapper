What can you see in the sky?

Pick one. You’ll point your phone at it and the app measures how high it is.

How to take it

  1. 1Hold your phone upright.
  2. 2Line the Sun up inside the circle.
  3. 3Hold still. The reading is taken for you.

Look at the screen, never straight at the Sun.

Ready?

Your phone will ask for three things. Tap Allow on each.

Camerato see the Sun
Motionto measure the angle
Locationto place you on the map

Saved: the height, the time, your phone type and your location to about 1 km. No name, no photo.

Check your phone’s level

Most phones’ tilt sensors are a little off. This measures by how much, so readings come out right. It takes about ten seconds.

  1. Lay the phone screen-up on a table.
  2. Tap Reading 1 and do not touch it for two seconds.
  3. Keep it flat with the screen facing up, spin it half a turn like a compass needle, and tap Reading 2. Don’t flip it over.
