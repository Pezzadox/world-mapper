No account or login is needed: open the app and tap through the three steps.

To review the app indoors, where the Sun is not visible, the aim check can be skipped: after ten seconds a "Take the reading now" button appears, which records whatever angle the phone is held at. Any bright round light source (a lamp) will also satisfy the automatic detection.

Permissions: the camera is used only to aim at the Sun or Moon on screen and no image is stored or transmitted; the motion sensors measure the angle above level; location is rounded to about 1 km before it is saved. Readings are stored anonymously, with no name, account or device identifier.

This app is an open scientific experiment and takes no position on the question it measures.
